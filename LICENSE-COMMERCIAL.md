## What counts as commercial use

Commercial use is any use that is not a *permitted purpose* under the
PolyForm Noncommercial License — broadly, any use intended for or
directed toward commercial advantage or private monetary compensation.

The following remain **free** under the PolyForm Noncommercial License
and do **not** need a commercial license:

- personal projects, hobby use, private study and experimentation;
- research, experiment, and testing for the benefit of public
  knowledge;
- use by charitable organizations, educational institutions, public
  research organizations, public safety/health organizations,
  environmental organizations, and government institutions —
  regardless of their source of funding.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask before you rely
on the software — see the contact below.
